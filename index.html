About Us

About AADYA ENT & CARDIAC CLINIC

AADYA ENT & CARDIAC CLINIC is a reputable healthcare provider in Dehradun, specializing in ENT and Cardiology services. The clinic is run by Dr. NK Belwal, an ENT specialist, and Chandra Mohan Belwal, a cardiologist, both committed to delivering patient-centered care.

From ear, nose and throat conditions to heart health, the clinic is designed to provide reliable consultation and diagnostic support in a comfortable setting.

  • Specialized ENT & Cardiac consultation
  • Modern diagnostic support
  • Patient-first, comfortable experience
  • Located at Mothorowala Rd, Bengali Kothi, Tehri Nagar, Dehradun
